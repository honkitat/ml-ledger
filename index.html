Verdict

The return is real and official, but the last official print is +785.8% (30 Sep), not “+800–900% today”, and he is −8.5% from his July peak. July alone produced 51.5% of the year’s gain.

Unresolved inconsistency

The published dataset reports +300.1R for 2026. At the stated 0.3% risk per trade that compounds to only +146%, not +786%. Producing 8.86× from 300.1R requires 0.727% per R — 2.4× the stated risk unit. Either R is measured against something other than account equity, or real risk on winners was far above 0.3%.

Method & limits

  • Source: archived stream transcripts (7 Jan – 4 Nov 2026), parsed into trade events; every event carries a verbatim quote string-matched back into the transcript ( verified).
  • Tickers: resolved by the extractor, a further 61 recovered by a model repair pass, the rest dropped as sector or commodity words.
  • Price context: the stream date's daily OHLC. It brackets his fill; it is not his fill.
  • Screen evidence: video is obtainable (fetch_loader_video.py via loader.to bypasses the platform's datacenter-IP wall — EP115 pulled at 360p, 90 MB). What is still missing is a frame where an IBKR positions/orders panel is visible: the sampled frames are TradingView charts plus his watchlist, so fills, stop distances and per-trade size remain unverified. The watchlist panel is already captured daily by the stream pipeline and logged in the journal's Watchlist tab.
